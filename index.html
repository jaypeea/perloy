RECORD STRUCTURE

Data dictionary

Five tables based on section 5.2.3.

Field handling: Product SKUs use the SKU- prefix and a unique number you enter. The internal product ID remains fixed to preserve sales and stock links. Sales and supply receipts save their exact recorded time. Customer names are saved on each sale. Supplier contact numbers are stored as text so leading zeroes are preserved.
